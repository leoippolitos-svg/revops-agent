## Rules for third-party material

- Copy only what its license allows, keep its license file next to it, and list it here with the source and the exact version.
- Never edit it in place. Repository-specific rules live in our own files (for skills, `.claude/skills/create-skill/` applies this repository's rules on top of `skill-creator`). An update replaces the whole folder with a newer pinned version, in its own pull request.
- Rule zero still applies: nothing from any employer or client, whatever its license.
