# Statistical check

## Steps

1. Get the counts behind the rate, not only the rate.
2. With counts under about 30, say plainly that one record moves the rate a lot.
3. Use a simple test and say which: for monthly counts, how often a value this low or high happens at the recent average rate; for two rates, the difference against its sampling range.
4. Report it as "within normal swing" or "outside it", never as proof of a cause.
5. Say what more data would settle it: one or two more months, or a larger group.

## Example

"Is December's drop real?" "Three won deals against an average of 5.2: a month with three or fewer happens about one month in four at that rate, so it is within normal swing."

## Troubleshooting

- **The test says significant but the business disagrees**: check the method first (join, filter, window) before the business.
