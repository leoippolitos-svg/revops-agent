# Forecast revenue

## Steps

1. Read `mrr`, `funnel-stages` and `okrs`. State the target if one exists.
2. Fix the anchors first: the last full month's ending MRR, and the history of new and churned MRR. A projection that does not reproduce the last full month is wrong.
3. Build the projection from three parts, each written down: open pipeline times the win rate of mature cohorts (with its range), the subscription lag of up to two weeks, and churn at the recent monthly rate.
4. Give a low, middle and high case from the range of win rates; never one number.
5. Label whose projection it is (the agent's, from these assumptions) and keep it apart from any official plan.
6. Say what would change it: a large deal, a churn, a shift in win rate.

## Example

"Will we reach the MRR target?" "From $125,099 today, the middle case ends the year at about X with the range Y to Z, against the $250,000 target; the assumptions are listed below."

## Troubleshooting

- **The forecast is far above history**: check that bookings were divided by 12; deal value is yearly.
- **Too few cohorts**: say the range is wide because history is short.
