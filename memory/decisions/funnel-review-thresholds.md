# Funnel-review thresholds accepted as they are

## Decision
The funnel review names a constraint only for a conversion drop of at least 5 points weighted by volume, or for time in stage growing at least 50% and 3 days. Qualification is owned by the SDR team, every later stage by Sales.

## Why
They were the builder's assumptions, not documented rules. The owner reviewed them and accepted them as they are until real data shows otherwise.

## What it changes for the agent
Do not suggest other thresholds as if they were open; say these are accepted assumptions when a review is discussed.
