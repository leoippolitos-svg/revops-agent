# dbt gold layer and the first context documents

## What was done
Added four gold models (`mrr_monthly`, `active_customer_monthly`, `funnel_stage_monthly`, `mql_monthly`) with catalog descriptions and tests, a month-spine macro, and 8 gold tests in `tests/test_dbt_layers.py`. Wrote the context documents for the concepts they introduce (`mrr`, `funnel-stages`, `mql`) and rewrote `active-customer` so its annotations point to real models. `context/index.yaml` is updated.

## Why
Gold is the layer the agent reads, and the standard asks for a context document whenever a gold model introduces a concept. The documents say what the numbers mean; the catalog says what the columns are.

## Result or decision
- `dbt build` passes 95 models and tests. The Python tests recompute key figures straight from silver instead of trusting gold: ending MRR of the last month equals the subscriptions still in force, starting plus new minus churned equals ending in every month, active customers are counted per company and only after a first payment, funnel entries add up to the stage history, and MQL counts add up while the disagreements stay visible.
- "As of" is the date of the newest daily export in bronze, so every month ends on the last day of data and the last month is partial.
- MRR counts a subscription from its start; an active customer also needs a first payment. The two differ on purpose and the documents say so.
- `active-customer` is narrower than its first draft: test accounts, trials and payment grace periods are not modeled because Fernway's data has none, and the document says so instead of promising them.
- No expansion or contraction in MRR: the generator never changes a subscription's price.
- Open decisions are written into the documents for the owner, not decided silently: which MQL flag is canonical, and the entry and exit criteria of each funnel stage.
- All four documents stay `draft` until the owner reviews them in Phase 2. Every annotation was checked against the dbt catalog with a throwaway script; a permanent check belongs in CI (next PR).

## Rules and context changed
- `context/concepts/active-customer.md` — annotations now point to real models; rules narrowed to what the data supports.
- `context/concepts/mrr.md` — new, draft.
- `context/concepts/funnel-stages.md` — new, draft; stage criteria still open.
- `context/concepts/mql.md` — new, draft; canonical flag still open.
